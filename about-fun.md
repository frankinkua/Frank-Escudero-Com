# About-fun — the human side

The site is Frank's CV, but a CV only tells you the technical story. Here is
the rest — all of it true, none of it on the résumé.

## The origin story
- A lifelong autodidact: he was taking apart RC cars to build radios before the
  internet existed.
- Coded a custom 3D physics game engine **before age 12**, teaching himself the
  advanced math and physics along the way (when a physics professor couldn't
  help with the programming side, Frank decoded the reflection math himself).
- Offered to skip grades in school, stayed in normal classes, and turned that
  boredom into a drive to dismantle and rebuild complex systems.

## Fun facts
- Unusually unenthusiastic about football for an Argentine — yet surprisingly
  flexible and open-minded for a German.
- As a 9-year-old he worked as an actor in the film **Seven Years in Tibet**
  alongside Brad Pitt. His character was ultimately cut from the movie, but he
  kept the autograph (and the memory of Brad Pitt trying to hit on his mom).
- Five-time startup founder, across Germany, the US and Argentina.
- Certified Taekwondo trainer; experienced in tennis, golf, horseback riding,
  archery, violin, dance and acting.

## What drives him
- **The fight against entropy:** believes all systems — biological, social,
  corporate — naturally degrade. Maintenance isn't enough; a system needs an
  "Active Architect" constantly redesigning it. His guiding quote: *"Every
  moment I'm not pushing myself working on a skill, I'm slowly losing it."*
- **Justice & systemic integrity:** deeply sensitive to inefficiencies,
  corruption and power imbalances — from fighting to protect ants as a kid to
  fighting systemic exploitation in adult ecosystems.
- **Technology as empowerment:** convinced that *human-empowered AI workflows*
  will dominate the work environment, and that the goal is to empower people
  and organizations, not replace them.

## Passions
Environmental sustainability, mindfulness, investigative research, and ethical
tech deployment. Lives near Frankfurt with his partner.
