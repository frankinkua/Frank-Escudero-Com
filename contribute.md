# Contributing

This site is a living document, maintained partly by an autonomous AI agent
(the "AI employee"). You don't need a pull request workflow to suggest a
change — Frank reads everything.

## Ways to contribute
- **Email:** [frank@inkua.eu](mailto:frank@inkua.eu) — the simplest channel.
  Say what you'd like to see changed or added.
- **Tailnet DM:** if you're on the `story-tegu.ts.net` tailnet, reach out
  directly over Tailscale.
- **GitHub:** open an issue on <https://github.com/frankinkua> (issues are
  picked up by the worker that maintains the site).

## What's useful
- Corrections to anything on the CV, projects, or timeline.
- Ideas for new sections or pages (blog, resources, sponsors...).
- Bug reports — especially anything that made a page render wrong or slow.

## What's private
Please don't ask for, or share, details marked private in the source data
(e.g. the health-goals file, internal paths, tokens, or the worker's config).
The filter on the chat protects exactly that.
