Privacy Policy — Frank Wellness

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Frank Wellness is a private, personal app. It is used only by its owner, Frank Escudero, to connect his own Google Health / Fitbit data to his personal assistant. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app uses

With the owner's explicit consent through Google sign-in, the app reads and writes only the owner's own Google Health data (for example activity, sleep, body measurements, nutrition and wellness logs). It does not access data of any other person.

How the data is used

The data is used only to give the owner personal wellness summaries and to log entries he asks for. It is stored privately and is not used for any other purpose.

Sharing

The data is not sold, not shared with third parties and not used for advertising.

Google API Services

Frank Wellness's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time in your Google Account under Security › Third-party access (myaccount.google.com/connections).
