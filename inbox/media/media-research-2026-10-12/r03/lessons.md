# Research Lessons

* **Search Limitations:** The standard search engine tool returned inconclusive or no results for combinations of `"Asombro Extremo"`, specific campaign names, and awards like `"Cannes Lions"`.
* **Corporate Continuity & Rights:** Bounded fallback URLs revealed that Asombro Extremo operates as "Amaze" (Asombro Extremo LLC). Crucially, discovering the source must immediately be followed by a rights check (T&C Section 6.3 prohibited copying).
* **Detailed Award Verification:** When verifying awards, closely read the full list. An award trip to Cannes Lions was awarded at the Clarín 2015 event, but it was won by Blue Hive, not Asombro Extremo.

**Improved prompt clause:** If the standard search tool returns inconclusive or no results for known valid entities, provide an alternative research tool or pre-seed specific canonical domain URLs (e.g., Cannes Lions official archive link or the agency's exact URL) rather than relying solely on open web search discovery. Always confirm exact rights via T&C pages before downloading, and verify award attribution explicitly, avoiding assumption by proximity.
