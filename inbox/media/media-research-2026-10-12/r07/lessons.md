# Research Lessons

*   **Search Engine Scraping:** Automated attempts to scrape search engines (like DuckDuckGo HTML) are consistently blocked with anomaly detection/captcha forms. Standard curl and Python `urllib` requests are blocked.
*   **Alternative Discovery:** The Wikipedia API remains a reliable and accessible method for finding structured information and verifying claims when standard search engines are blocked.
*   **Prompt Clause Improvement:** "When performing public-source research, prioritize accessing structured APIs (like Wikipedia API) and known canonical domains directly, as general search engines will block automated retrieval attempts."
